# Legal/Compliance Document Analysis

## Overview
A specialized RAG system for analyzing contracts, policies, and regulatory documents. Extracts clauses, identifies risks, ensures compliance, and compares documents.

## Purpose
Demonstrates compliance-critical RAG:
- Contract clause extraction
- Risk identification
- Regulatory compliance checking
- Document comparison (vendor vs standard)
- Timeline extraction
- Obligation tracking
- Amendment detection

## Features
✅ Contract parsing and clause extraction
✅ Risk identification and scoring
✅ Regulatory compliance verification
✅ Document comparison and diff
✅ Timeline and deadline extraction
✅ Obligation tracking
✅ Amendment history tracking

## Testing Focus (Later)
- Clause extraction completeness
- Risk scoring accuracy
- False positive rate
- Compliance verification correctness
- Comparison accuracy
- Deadline capture accuracy
- Legal terminology handling
